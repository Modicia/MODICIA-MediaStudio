# MODICIA-MediaStudio
Multi-track audio and video editing suite for MODICIA O.S.

MODICIA MediaStudio is a modern video editor designed for high-performance video stream manipulation and processing. Built for creators and creative professionals, it delivers efficient workflow controls for managing video streams.

Developed by Marco M. Mariani for MODICIA O.S., MODICIA MediaStudio is released under the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPLv3) combined with the attribution terms of Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Features
Modern video stream manipulation and processing
Designed for high-performance creative workflows
Released under GPLv3 and CC BY 4.0 dual terms
Optimized for MODICIA O.S.
Ultra-fast, lossy and lossless video/audio editor, stream switcher & automatic scene detector for Linux.
Lossless Direct Stream Copy:** Remux, edit, and slice videos without re-encoding or losing original quality.
Pure Audio Extractor:** Export clean, uncorrupted PCM WAV, AAC, and MP3 tracks with dedicated channel isolation.
Automated Scene Detection:** Instantly analyze video changes and convert detected keyframes into timeline cut points or chapters.
Batch Scene Splitter:** One-click export of detected scenes into individual high-quality MJPEG video clips and matching WAV audio files.
MKV Multiplexer & Subtitle Tool:** Seamlessly manage multiple audio streams, external subtitle tracks, and custom chapter metadata.
Synchronized Dual Preview Canvas:** Side-by-side comparative inspection with real-time mouse-synced Zoom (6%–400%) and Pan controls.
Swiss Army Knife Export Profiles:** Flexible container routing supporting MP4, MKV, AVI, and raw audio extractions.
Available as native **.deb** packages and portable **.AppImage** builds.
